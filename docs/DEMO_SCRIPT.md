### Word-for-Word Presenter Script

#### 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:15)
> "Good morning, judges. Public services in India are powerful, but citizen access is hampered by complex circulars, superseded rules, and conflicting deadlines across central and state authorities.
> 
> Justice Vault is **not** another generic chatbot wrapper. It is an **evidence-grounded public-service intelligence layer** built on one core rule:
> **'No verified evidence → no confident answer.'**
> It complements portals like UMANG, DigiLocker, and myScheme by providing verifiable interpretation and actionable guidance."

#### 2. Primary Grounded Query & Evidence Trace (0:15 - 0:35)
> "Let's ask a real question: *'What documents do I need for PM Awas Yojana and what is the income limit?'*
> 
> Instantly, Justice Vault retrieves the MoHUA 2026 guidelines. Notice:
> 1. We receive a plain-language answer in English or Hindi.
> 2. The **Confidence Gate is HIGH (96%)**.
> 3. Under **Evidence Trace**, every single claim is individually grounded: we see the issuing ministry, effective date, section number, retrieved legal excerpt, official URL, and cryptographic SHA-256 digest."

#### 3. Deterministic Action Engine (0:35 - 0:50)
> "Instead of stopping at text, our Deterministic Action Engine compiles a checkable action plan.
> When the citizen clicks **'Open Official Route'**, our safety architecture requires **explicit confirmation**. 
> We never pretend to automatically submit government applications. We verify preparation and route citizens directly to the official portal."

#### 4. The Differentiator: Conflict Detection (0:50 - 1:05)
> "Now observe our defensive safety engine. What happens when official directives disagree?
> When a student asks: *'What is the deadline for the scholarship application?'*
> 
> Justice Vault flags **⚠ CONFLICT DETECTED**. 
> The Central Ministry of Social Justice specifies November 30, but the State Directorate mandates October 31.
> Rather than hallucinating a false resolution, confidence is downgraded to LOW, both sources are presented side-by-side, and clear cautionary guidance is given."

#### 5. Freshness Engine, Audit & Closing (1:05 - 1:30)
> "Similarly, our **Freshness Engine** detects when a citizen asks about outdated procedures—like 2024 paper migration slips—and alerts them to the active 2026 biometric e-PoS order.
> 
> Every transaction is recorded in our tamper-evident **Audit Trail**, and our **Benchmark Matrix** reflects 100% empirical grounding on our test suite.
> 
> Justice Vault brings truth, safety, and deterministic action to digital governance. Thank you!"
